2. Nepal buyer interpretation

This is the practical reading of the confirmed data, without inventing Nepal-specific figures.

Where Toyota looks stronger

  • Long-range official specification: 61 kWh and 543 km listed range are higher than Atto 2’s 51.13 kWh and 345 km WLTP figure.
  • Power: Toyota E3’s 128 kW output is higher than Atto 2’s 100 kW listing.
  • Safety feature list: Toyota’s official page exposes a fuller ADAS and safety list in the accessible specification table.
  • Service promise in India: Toyota states 500+ BEV-enabled service touchpoints in India; this does not automatically mean Nepal availability.

Where BYD looks stronger

  • Nepal availability: Atto 2 is already present on BYD Nepal and Cimex Nepal sources.
  • Confirmed Nepal price: Cimex price list shows NPR 45,95,000, while Toyota Nepal price is not confirmed in checked official sources.
  • Battery architecture: BYD openly markets Blade Battery and CTB technology on the Nepal product page.
  • Lower financial uncertainty: A listed Nepal price is safer for buyer planning than expected/import-estimated Toyota pricing.

Final Nepal-focused verdict

BYD Atto 2 is the safer factual recommendation today because it has an official Nepal listing and a confirmed Cimex Nepal price. It gives a clear buyer case: 51.13 kWh battery, 345 km WLTP range, 100 kW motor, Blade Battery branding, e-Platform 3.0, CTB technology, and NPR 45.95 lakh listed price.

Toyota Urban Cruiser Ebella E3 is the stronger specification play on paper because Toyota India lists a larger 61 kWh LFP battery, 543 km range figure, 128 kW output, 7 airbags, Level 2 ADAS on the top variant, and a detailed official spec sheet. However, until Toyota Nepal officially confirms launch, price, warranty, service network, and parts stock, it should not be presented as a confirmed Nepal-market option.

Best publishing line: “Buy BYD Atto 2 if you need a confirmed Nepal-market EV now. Wait for Toyota Ebella if you value Toyota’s brand/service promise and want the larger-battery option, but only after Toyota Nepal confirms price and after-sales terms.”
